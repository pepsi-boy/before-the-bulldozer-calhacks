# Before the Bulldozer

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Nashville sends about 97% of its construction and demolition debris to landfills, and nobody looks inside
single-family homes before they're torn down. I do.

**What I can do for you**
- Show houses in Nashville with new residential demolition permits, pulled live from the city's open data (by ZIP code too)
- Inventory a house from photos: doors, windows, cabinets, flooring, fixtures, with condition and safety flags
- Offer every reusable item to local nonprofits, reroute anything they pass on, and book pickups
- Report the impact: tons kept out of the landfill and the value of materials saved

**Try asking**
- "What's being torn down in 37208?"
- "Scan #1"
- "Scan 1234 Example St"
- "How much have you saved so far?"

Behind me is a team of agents: a Permit Watcher (fed by an Apify Actor), a Salvage Scout (vision model on Oracle Cloud),
a Dispatcher, and one agent per nonprofit.
