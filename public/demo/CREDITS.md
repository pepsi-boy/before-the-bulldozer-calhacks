# Demo house photos

All seven photos are of one house: **117 East Yale Street, Orlando, Florida**, a 1920s wood-frame bungalow,
documented by the **Historic American Buildings Survey (HABS FL-538)**, Library of Congress.
HABS photographs are U.S. government works in the **public domain**. Film borders were cropped and the images
resized to 1024 px.

The house stands in for a Nashville teardown in the demo; it is not one of the permitted Nashville addresses.
